How it works

1
Enter your data

Teachers, subjects, classes and rooms. Each subject needs at least one teacher. Each class needs subjects with weekly period counts.

2
Set rules

Working days, periods per day, teacher max load and available days. The engine respects every constraint you define.

3
Generate

The scheduler builds every required lesson, then places them slot by slot. It avoids teacher clashes, class clashes and room clashes, balances teacher load, and runs multiple passes to find the strongest fit.

4
Review and export

View by class or by teacher. Export CSV or print. Regenerate anytime after changing data.
